# Security Policy

Klaam processes voice and personal data. We take security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Email **security@klaamai.com**. Do NOT open a public issue.

- Acknowledgement within 48 hours.
- We aim to remediate confirmed vulnerabilities within 14 days.
- We commit to not pursuing legal action against good-faith security research.
- Reporters who wish to be credited are listed in our hall of fame (with consent).

## Scope
Klaam platform, SDKs, APIs, and infrastructure. Out of scope: third-party services we integrate (report to them directly).
