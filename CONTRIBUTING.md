# Contributing to Klaam

Thanks for your interest. Before contributing:

1. Open an issue to discuss significant changes before writing code.
2. Follow Conventional Commits (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, ...).
3. All commits must be signed off (DCO) — `git commit -s`.
4. Keep PRs focused and small; one concern per PR.
5. Ensure CI is green before requesting review.

By contributing, you agree your contributions are licensed under the project's license.
